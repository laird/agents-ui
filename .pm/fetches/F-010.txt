## Proposed Enhancement

Expand the existing \`revive_dropped_agents()\` and \`heal_all_workers()\` stubs into a comprehensive health-check and auto-recovery system, with a live health indicator rendered in the \`SwarmView\` header.

## Rationale

Multi-worker swarms are fragile: a worker can hang silently, a tmux pane can crash, or a status file can become stale after a machine sleep/wake cycle.
